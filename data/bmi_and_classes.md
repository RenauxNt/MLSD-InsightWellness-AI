# BMI and Obesity Classes

## Body Mass Index (BMI)
BMI = weight (kg) / height² (m²)

## Categories
| BMI Range | Category |
|----------|--------|
| < 18.5 | Underweight |
| 18.5 – 24.9 | Normal weight |
| 25 – 29.9 | Overweight |
| 30 – 34.9 | Obesity Type I |
| 35 – 39.9 | Obesity Type II |
| ≥ 40 | Obesity Type III |

## Obesity Class Definitions

### Insufficient Weight
Low body mass relative to height.

### Normal Weight
Healthy weight range associated with lowest metabolic risk.

### Overweight Level I
Early excess weight gain with increased risk of metabolic disease.

### Overweight Level II
Higher excess weight, closer to obesity threshold.

### Obesity Type I
First stage of clinical obesity with significant health risk.

### Obesity Type II
Severe obesity with increased risk of cardiovascular disease.

### Obesity Type III
Extreme obesity (morbid obesity), highest health risk category.

## Limitations of BMI
- Does not account for muscle mass
- May misclassify athletes
