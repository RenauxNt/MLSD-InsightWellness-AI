# Obesity Overview

## Definition
Obesity is a medical condition characterized by excessive body fat accumulation that presents a risk to health.

## Causes
- Genetic factors
- Poor diet (high calorie intake)
- Sedentary lifestyle
- Hormonal imbalances

## Risks
- Cardiovascular diseases
- Type 2 diabetes
- Joint problems
- Reduced life expectancy

## Prevention
- Balanced diet
- Regular physical activity
- Behavioral changes
